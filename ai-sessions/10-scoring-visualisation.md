## Claude

I added an explanation box to every chart: which way the factor pulls the score (higher or lower) and how strongly. The regenerated images and the script are committed to `feat/t02-scoring` (`c94f253`), not pushed. I looked at all the images, nothing overlaps.

| Chart | Direction | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Skiing: snowfall | more hours of snowfall give a lower score | up to 30 % of the score |
| Skiing: wind | stronger wind gives a lower score | up to 28 % |
| Skiing: temperature | best near 3 °C, colder or warmer gives a lower score | up to 22 % |
| Skiing: cloud cover | more cloud gives a lower score | up to 19 %, the weakest factor |
| Skiing: rain | more rain gives a lower score | multiplies the whole score: 1.5 mm is ×0.5, 3 mm is 0 |
| Skiing: snow depth | deeper gives a higher score, up to 0.3 m | multiplies the whole score: 0.15 m is ×0.5 |
| Surfing | height 1–2 m is best, longer period gives a higher score, stronger wind gives a lower one | exponents 0.35, 0.35 and 0.30: halving the utility lowers the score by 21 %, 21 % and 19 %. A zero in any factor gives a score of 0 |
| Outdoor: comfort (humidex) | best at humidex 23–26, lower otherwise | from 0 to +0.40, the strongest factor |
| Outdoor: cloud cover | more cloud gives a lower score | from +0.02 to +0.20 |
| Outdoor: precipitation | more precipitation gives a lower score | from +0.30 (dry) to −0.03 (25 mm and more) |
| Outdoor: wind | stronger wind gives a lower score | only ±0.10, the weakest factor (that is why the cap is needed) |

- **Outside the boxes:** the caps chart is labelled to say that they only lower the score and never raise it. The indoor chart is labelled to say that a better outdoor gives a lower indoor (−0.05 for every +0.1). On the humidex map and the surfing heat map, the explanation is added to the colour scale label.
- **Layout:** so that the boxes do not cover the curves, I raised the top of the Y axis on the charts with boxes. Because of this, the lower part of the axis does not reach the top of the frame; this is intentional. I removed the legend from the surfing charts, because the labels are clear anyway.
- **"Strength" in percent:** this is the maximum contribution of the factor to the score, that is, its weight. For skiing (where the weights sum to 1) it is an honest share. For outdoor these are absolute HCI points divided by 100.
